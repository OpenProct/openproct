# OpenProct

An open-source alternative for traditional proctoring services.

## Why OpenProct?

Current proctoring solutions are invasive, expensive, and lock you into proprietary systems. We believe there's a better way.

**Open source exam proctoring. Self-hosted. Privacy-first.**

## Features

- 🔓 **Open Source**: Full transparency and community-driven development
- 🏠 **Self-Hosted**: Complete control over your data and infrastructure
- 🔒 **Privacy-First**: Respect for student privacy and data protection
- 💰 **Cost-Effective**: No expensive licensing fees or per-student charges
